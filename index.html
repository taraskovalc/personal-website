01 Live

Slotted

Your Western timetable, in your calendar, in about five minutes.

Why I built it

A lot of students I know type every class into their calendar by hand. I went looking for a tool instead, but the one I found felt sketchy: it had me do steps I didn’t understand, and I ended up with a calendar that worked and no idea how. I wanted something free and clear.

What it does

Pick your calendar app and Slotted walks you through every step, from saving your schedule to adding it. Every class for the term appears, with reading weeks and holidays skipped. No account, nothing to install.

How it’s built

A plain JavaScript website on GitHub Pages, talking to a Node.js API and PostgreSQL database that run in Docker on my home server.

  • 335classes from one file
  • 31holiday classes skipped
  • 0typed in by hand

  • Node.js
  • Express
  • PostgreSQL

02 In progress

Arduino Telescope Tracker

A motorized mount that turns with the night sky, so the moon stays in view.

The Earth spins, so anything in the sky drifts out of a telescope in minutes. This mount will turn at exactly the sky’s speed to cancel that out, built cheaply from salvaged parts.

  1. Salvaged a stepper motor from an old printer
  2. Wired it to a TMC2130 driver (SPI) on a 12 V supply
  3. First powered test: the motor runs
  4. Turn at the sky’s speed: one full turn every 23 h 56 min
  5. Build the mount and track the moon

  • C++
  • Electronics
